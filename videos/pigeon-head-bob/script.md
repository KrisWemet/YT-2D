# Why do pigeons bob their heads?

`videos/pigeon-head-bob/` · script v1 · ~24.9 s · 67 words · status: published

## Voice-over

> A walking pigeon's head never actually moves backward.
> It freezes in place while the body moves forward, then darts ahead.
> You'd think that's for balance.
> But when scientists put pigeons on a treadmill, the bobbing stopped.
> Their legs kept walking, but their view never changed.
> So it's mostly about vision.
> Each freeze gives a steady view,
> like holding a camera still,
> which probably helps them spot movement.
