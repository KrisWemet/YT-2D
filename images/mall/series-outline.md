# The Night Guard: series outline

*The dead got in. He didn't get out.*

A stick-figure survival series told like a book. Each long video is one chapter of about 40 minutes. Each chapter splits into about 14 Shorts, and every Short works on its own.

## The pitch

A night guard with a key to every store gets locked inside his mall the night the dead rise. His seven-year-old daughter is a mile and a half away across the highway and blinks a flashlight at him every night. The woman on the radio wants the one key he was told never to use.

- **Series question:** will Danny get back to his daughter?
- **Book One question:** can he last thirty days inside Maplecrest Mall, and what does the last key open?
- **Why it can keep going:**
  - 42 keys means 42 doors, so every chapter can open a new one.
  - The nightly light from Lily's window is a heartbeat viewers check every chapter.
  - Book One ends with the villain driving away with the person Danny loves most.
- **How it's told:**
  - Danny tells it looking back, plainly and a little scared, which lets him drop flash-forwards ("In thirty days, I'd find out what it opens").
  - Cold opens jump to the present ("Right now...").
  - Violence stays implied, which suits the stick figures and keeps it ad-friendly.
- **Title:** the series is *The Night Guard*. The pilot's "The Night Guard Had Every Key" works as Chapter 1's video title.

### Book One at a glance: *Forty-Two Keys* (30 days in the mall)
1. **The King of the Mall** (night 1). His best friend dies at his gate, and a stranger on the radio sees him signal his daughter.
2. **The Boy in the Ceiling** (days 2–5). A runaway has been living in the mall for months, and the dead were called there on purpose.
3. **The Pharmacy** (days 6–10). Danny walks out among the dead for someone else, and his daughter's light blinks SOS.
4. **Across the Highway** (days 11–16). A deal with the enemy, a drone, and his first look at Lily in eleven days.
5. **The Fall of the King** (days 17–25). Vera takes the keys, the mall falls, and the light stops.
6. **Key Forty-Two** (days 26–30). The door he was told never to open, one last round with Marcus, and the woman who got to Lily first.

## Danny's journey

**At the start:**
- Danny Ruiz, 38, is the night guard at Maplecrest Mall and a single dad.
- He and Lily live with his mom on the tenth floor of the Parkview Apartments, right across the highway from the mall.
- He's decent, funny, and a little scared of everything.
- All his life he's picked the safe option and called it being a good dad. His job is literally locking doors.

**What he believes:** "Lock it and walk away. If the door's shut, nobody gets hurt." And: "I'm not a hero. I'm just the guy who holds the keys."

**What he wants:** to get home to Lily.

**What he needs:** to stop hiding behind locked doors. He has to let people in, take responsibility for them, and go first when he's scared. He has to become the king Lily already thinks he is.

**The wound:** on night one, his best friend begs him to open the gate. He has the key in his hand, and he doesn't open it.

**One door per chapter:**
1. He locks the gate on Marcus. *(He hides.)*
2. He lets a runaway kid stay in his store. *(The first door he opens, a small one.)*
3. He walks out among the dead for someone else, and opens the cage of the puppy Lily loved. *(A door that could have stayed shut.)*
4. He makes a deal with the woman on the radio to save his mom, and trusts one of her crew. *(A door to his enemy.)*
5. The doors he opened get a friend killed. He loses the keys and locks himself away. *(Rock bottom.)*
6. He opens the gate he wouldn't open on night one and walks out to Marcus. Then he opens the door he was told never to open, takes everyone through it, and leaves the key in the lock. *(He doesn't need the keys anymore.)*

**Where Book One leaves him:** "Lily was right about me. Just not about the keys."

## The cast

- **Lily (7).**
  - She draws pictures ("MY DAD IS THE KING OF THE MALL") and visits the same pet-store puppy every Saturday.
  - Every night at nine she blinks her lamp at the mall three times. It means "I love you."
  - She's offscreen for most of Book One, so every glimpse counts.
- **Lita (Danny's mom, 70).**
  - Tough and funny, and a type-1 diabetic with about ten days of insulin.
  - Her first text: "Lily is with me. Door locked. Do NOT come."
- **Marcus (22).**
  - Danny's best friend, the garage guard, studying to be a nurse.
  - He tells a terrible joke on the radio every night at one, and he owes Danny twenty bucks.
  - He dies at Danny's gate on night one and keeps walking the rounds afterward, his radio still clipped to his chest.
  - His nursing textbook saves a life in Chapter 3, and Danny finally gives him peace in Chapter 6.
- **Vera (40s): the woman on the radio.**
  - Calm, warm and funny, and she never raises her voice.
  - She leads a crew of thieves who hid in the department store after closing to break through a door marked B3.
  - To her, everyone is a key. The v2 pilot already promised she'd be "the most dangerous person in it."
- **Boone (50s).** Vera's muscle. He says almost nothing and is loyal to her, until he hits a line he won't cross.
- **Kip (24).** Vera's tech guy, who handles the drills, cameras and radios. Nervous, decent, and in over his head, he switches to Danny's side in Chapter 4.
- **Theo (15).**
  - A runaway who has secretly lived inside the mall for four months, in the ceiling above the cinema.
  - He knows every vent, crawlspace and roof hatch, and he doesn't trust adults.
  - He has been watching Danny for months: "You sing in the freight elevator."
- **Pancake.** The pet-store puppy Lily named, whom nobody ever bought. One bark can get everyone killed, so he's tension on four legs. Rule: the dog never dies.
- **Ana and Luis.** A night cleaner and her grown son, found hiding in the department store by Vera's crew. Vera treats them as staff; they end up as Danny's people.
- **Walt (offscreen).** The old night guard who handed Danny the keys: "That last one, you never use. Don't ask." He speaks through his old logbook in Chapter 6.

## World rules (all shown in Chapter 1, never broken)

1. They follow sound. Quiet keeps you alive, which is why the whispers matter in the voice.
2. Alone they're slow. Together they push like a crowd: glass gives, and steel gates hold, for now.
3. In silence they go back to their habits. Marcus still walks his rounds and rattles every gate.
4. A bite brings a fever, and the end within an hour.
5. The mall's backup generator runs the emergency lights, cameras and freezers, and has fuel for about three weeks.

## The series engine

These five things recur in every chapter:
- **The calendar.** A "DAY 6 OF 30" tag on screen, so any Short tells you where you are.
- **The door of the chapter.** Every chapter opens at least one new store with one of the 42 keys. Something they need is behind it, and something dangerous is in front of it.
- **Nine o'clock.** Lily's three blinks from the tenth floor. Any change is an instant hook: no light, a different signal, or someone else blinking back.
- **The radio.** Threats and twists come in on the security channel. Vera lives there.
- **Marcus's rounds.** The rattle of gates in the dark means he's passing. He's guilt, a clock, and in the finale, a tool.

Every chapter follows the same shape:
1. Cold open: jump to the chapter's scariest moment, then rewind.
2. Pick up the last chapter's cliffhanger.
3. Status: the day, what's running out, and how many are outside.
4. The door of the chapter: a new store with something they need behind it.
5. A people problem: Vera, a newcomer, or a secret.
6. Around minute 20, a reveal that changes the goal.
7. Around minutes 28 to 35, the big set piece.
8. Danny's choice: lock the door or open it.
9. Nine o'clock: the light, or no light.
10. A cliffhanger that asks the next chapter's question.

## How every chapter splits into Shorts

Each chapter is 14 segments of about 2½ to 3 minutes, and every segment is built to be cut into a Short:
1. **Hook (first 2 seconds):** one line that works for someone who has never seen the show.
2. **Anchor (first 5 seconds):** the on-screen tag ("DAY 6 OF 30 · MAPLECREST MALL") and, when it fits, one spoken line: "I'm the night guard. The dead got in. I didn't get out."
3. **One problem and one turn.** The middle can be trimmed to make a 60–90 second Short without losing the story.
4. **Cliff (last line):** end on a new question, never an answer. The next segment opens with the answer.

Hook rules:
- The first line never depends on an earlier Short: "My best friend called," not "Marcus called."
- Never open with "So," "Then" or "Previously."
- Title each Short by its hook ("He begged me to open the gate"), not "Part 7."
- At real decision points, ask the viewer ("Which store would you pick?", "Would you have opened the gate?"). The comments will argue.
- Every Short ends on a card: "The full chapter is on the channel."
- One chapter gives you two weeks of daily Shorts, each pointing back to the chapter.

Hook types to rotate:
- **Right now:** "Right now there's a zombie pressed up against my gate."
- **Confession:** "I had the key in my hand. I didn't open it."
- **Impossible fact:** "My best friend died last night. This morning, he's still doing his rounds."
- **Countdown:** "I had four minutes to pick the one store I'd survive in."
- **Flash-forward:** "In thirty days, I'd find out what the last key opens."
- **Number:** "Forty-two keys. One door nobody's allowed to open."

## Book One: *Forty-Two Keys*

### Chapter 1: The King of the Mall (night 1 to night 2)

All 14 segments, each with the hook that opens its Short and the line it ends on.

**1. The Gate** *(cold open, 2:45 a.m.)*
- **Hook:** "Right now there's a zombie pressed up against my gate, and it's wearing my best friend's uniform."
- The radio on his chest is about to tell every one of them where I'm hiding. An hour ago, I was basically the king of this mall.
- **Cliff:** "Let me back up."

**2. Forty-Two Keys**
- **Hook:** "I have a key to every store in this mall. The man who gave them to me said never to use the last one."
- The setup:
  - I'm Danny, the night guard. I work nights so I'm at the bus stop at 3:15 when Lily gets off.
  - We live with my mom on the tenth floor of the Parkview Apartments, right across the highway.
  - Every night at nine, Lily blinks her lamp at the mall three times. It means "I love you," and I blink my flashlight back.
  - I taught her one more signal, SOS, for emergencies. She thinks it's a game.
- The keys:
  - There are forty-two of them.
  - Old Walt, who had the job before me, handed them over and tapped the last one: "That one, you never use. Don't ask."
  - Its tag says B3. There is no B3 on the mall map.
- **Cliff:** "Tonight, I'd only need one of them. In thirty days, I'd find out what the last one opens."

**3. One-Forty**
- **Hook:** "Every night at one a.m., my best friend tells me a terrible joke on the radio. Tonight, he whispered."
- Marcus guards the parking garage. He's 22, studying to be a nurse, and owes me twenty bucks.
- Tonight's joke, at one on the dot, is about zombies.
- At 1:40 he's back: "Danny. There's a guy on the ramp. He's walking... wrong." Then: "Sir? Sir, you're bleeding..."
- **Cliff:** "Then the radio did something I'd never heard it do. It went completely silent."

**4. Camera Six**
- **Hook:** "I watched my best friend get attacked on a security camera. I didn't go down there."
- Garage, level two. Marcus is on the ground.
- Behind him, the ramp is filling up from the highway: six, ten, thirty.
- Marcus, barely: "Don't come down. Lock up."
- They all walk the same way.
- Camera 14, the hallway behind the food court, is black. It's been out since Tuesday, and I put in a ticket. *(Plant: that's where the thieves were.)*
- **Cliff:** "They were all heading for the food court. And the food court is made of glass."

**5. Four Minutes**
- **Hook:** "I had four minutes to pick the one store I'd survive in. Which one would you pick?"
- Malls are built to let people in. But every store has a steel gate. The choices:
  - The pharmacy: medicine, but all glass.
  - The food court: food, but that's where they're headed.
  - The department store: beds, and nine entrances.
  - Summit Outdoors: tents, water filters, bats, and one way in.
- **Cliff:** "Behind me, the food court doors exploded."

**6. Key Thirty-One**
- **Hook:** "I locked myself in a sporting goods store with the dead right outside. Then somebody knocked."
- Under the gate, pull it down, lock it with key 31.
- I sit in the dark between the tents with a hand over my mouth, listening to the shuffling.
- Then our knock, the one Marcus and I use on the office door. "Danny. It's me."
- He knew which store I'd pick. He's gray, and his sleeve is soaked.
- **Cliff:** "I had the key in my hand."

**7. The Last Joke**
- **Hook:** "My best friend begged me to open the gate. I had the key in my hand. I didn't open it."
- The bite on his arm. Dozens of them at the end of the hall. My hand shaking on the lock.
- Marcus watches my face and gets it: "Hey. It's okay. I wouldn't open it either."
- He sits with his back against the bars, and we whisper for forty minutes.
- "Tell Lily the king's still in his castle."
- One more terrible joke. "And Danny? I still owe you twenty bucks."
- **Cliff:** "At 2:31, Marcus stopped breathing. At 2:39, he stood up."

**8. The Radio**
- **Hook:** "There's a dead man at my gate, and the radio on his chest is about to get me killed."
- My radio crackles. A woman, whispering: "Is anyone still in the mall? I'm on the second floor of the department store. Please. I'm not alone up here."
- Her voice comes out of my radio, then out of Marcus's one second later.
- Every head in the hallway turns. Forty of them. One gate.
- **Cliff:** "The gate started to bend. And she was about to call again."

**9. Through the Bars**
- **Hook:** "To stay alive, I had to reach through the bars and take the radio off my dead best friend's chest."
- I switch mine off, but his keeps going.
- His teeth are an inch from my wrist. The clip sticks: he always wore it upside down, and I told him a hundred times.
- I get it, and stuff it deep in a sleeping bag.
- Without the noise, they forget me and drift away. All except Marcus.
- **Cliff:** "Then, from inside the sleeping bag, she said my name."

**10. She Knew My Name**
- **Hook:** "A stranger on the radio knew my name. Then she asked about my keys."
- I'd squeezed the talk button grabbing it. "Danny, right? The night guard. I can hear you breathing."
- She doesn't sound scared anymore: "You've got all the keys, don't you? Get some sleep. We'll talk tomorrow."
- The mall closes at nine. I walked every hallway at midnight. Nobody was in here.
- **Cliff:** "So who was she? And what was she doing in my mall at two in the morning?"

**11. Rounds**
- **Hook:** "My best friend died last night. This morning, he's still doing his rounds."
- Gray light through the skylights. In the quiet, they just stand there.
- Then: rattle, rattle. Marcus is walking the loop, shaking every gate the way he did every night.
- The night cleaner is still pushing her cart into a wall.
- The rule I learn on day one: they follow sound, and in silence they go back to their habits.
- **Cliff:** "Then my phone buzzed. One message, sent at 2:14 a.m."

**12. Thirty Days**
- **Hook:** "On the first morning of the end of the world, I got one text."
- From my mom: "Lily is with me. Door locked. Do NOT come."
- On a camping radio, an emergency broadcast: "Shelter in place. Rescue teams will reach every area within thirty days."
- I count what I've got: 72 bottles of water, 40 protein bars, freeze-dried meals, a camp stove, water filters and bats. That's water for twelve days.
- And my mom's diabetic. Her insulin lasts about ten.
- **Cliff:** "Rescue was thirty days away. My mom's insulin would run out in ten."

**13. The Roof**
- **Hook:** "From the roof of this mall, I can see my daughter's window. It might as well be the moon."
- Up the stockroom ladder and through the hatch.
- Eight lanes of abandoned cars, with the dead walking between them. Smoke. A helicopter that doesn't stop.
- Parkview, a mile and a half away: tenth floor, fourth window from the left.
- Why I can't just go: my car's in the garage with them, there are eight lanes of the dead, and I'd lead them straight to her door.
- **Cliff:** "At 8:59, the whole city went dark. Including her window."

**14. Three Blinks**
- **Hook:** "Every night at nine, my daughter blinks a light at me three times. Tonight the whole city was dark."
- 9:00. Nothing. 9:01. Then one, two, three: a flashlight in the tenth-floor window.
- She's alive. I blink back three times and can't stop shaking.
- Then the radio in my pocket clicks on. It's her again: "That's sweet, Danny."
- **Cliff:** "Who's in the window?"

### Chapter 2: The Boy in the Ceiling (days 2–5)

- **Cold open:** "Right now my leg is hanging through the ceiling over a hundred dead people, and my best friend is walking right under it."
- **Picks up:** Danny never answers "Who's in the window?" He tells Vera nothing about Lily, ever.
- **Story:**
  - Vera calls every night, warm and funny. She says she's a shopper who fell asleep in a fitting room, and that there's a little girl up there with her who hasn't eaten. Both are lies.
  - Meanwhile, food keeps disappearing from Danny's locked store. He dusts the floor with climbing chalk, and the footprints go up the wall to the ceiling.
  - He waits up and catches a kid dropping through a ceiling tile. It's Theo, fifteen, who has been secretly living in the mall for four months.
- **Danny's choice:**
  - His instinct is to throw Theo out. He couldn't protect Marcus, so how can he protect a kid?
  - But his water runs out in ten days, and Theo knows where eight thousand gallons are: the center court fountain. You can tap it from the pump room underneath, and you reach that through the ceiling.
  - They make a deal. By the end of the chapter, Danny lets him stay.
- **Set piece:** the crawl over the center court. A tile cracks and Danny's leg drops through, above the dead, just as the rattle of Marcus's rounds comes down the hall. Theo hauls him up.
- **Reveal:** in the pump room, Theo tells him about night one. "The drill people. Three of them, cutting through the wall behind the pretzel place at 1:30. Loud as a chainsaw. That's what brought them." The dead were called, and Marcus died because of Vera's crew.
- **Ends:**
  - At nine, Lily blinks and Danny answers.
  - Then a light on the department store roof blinks three times at Lily's window.
  - Theo has one more thing: "They weren't robbing a store. They were trying to get down to a door with no sign on it. B3." Danny's hand goes to the last key.
- **Shorts hooks:**
  - "Somebody's been secretly living in my mall for four months. I'm the night guard, and I never noticed."
  - "To get water, I had to crawl through the ceiling right over their heads."
  - "The dead didn't come to my mall by accident. Somebody called them."
  - "A stranger just blinked a light at my daughter's window."

### Chapter 3: The Pharmacy (days 6–10)

- **Cold open:** "I'm standing in the middle of a hundred dead people in my socks, and the puppy behind me is about to bark."
- **Story:**
  - Theo cut his leg in the crawlspace. By day seven it's infected and he's burning up.
  - The pharmacy has antibiotics, and insulin for Danny's mom. But it's across the center court with a glass front, and Danny has never set foot on the floor.
  - In Marcus's locker he finds Marcus's nursing textbook, with jokes scribbled in the margins, and a folded twenty with a note: "Told you I'd pay you back." The book tells him which antibiotic to use and how much.
- **Danny's choice:** he goes out, for someone else.
- **Set piece:**
  - A silent walk at noon through the standing dead, timed to Marcus's rounds so the rattling pulls their heads the other way.
  - Next to the pharmacy, in the pet store, Pancake starts to whine. He's the puppy Lily visited every Saturday.
  - The dead turn toward the pet store. Leaving Pancake would save Danny. He opens the cage.
- **Twist:** it goes loud. A road flare lands far down the hallway, thrown from the department store balcony, and the dead follow it. Vera saved him. "Now you owe me, Danny." She wants to meet face to face.
- **The meeting:**
  - Through a gate, Vera is warm, funny and completely normal. She offers to join forces: his keys, her people.
  - As they leave, her tech guy Kip slips Danny a note: "She'll kill you for that key."
- **Ends:**
  - Theo's fever breaks.
  - At nine, Lily's light blinks: short short short, long long long, short short short. "Every night my daughter blinks I love you. Tonight she blinked SOS."
  - Danny knows what it means. Mom's insulin is gone, and the new insulin is in his hand, a highway away.
- **Shorts hooks:**
  - "My best friend's been dead for a week. Tonight he saved a kid's life."
  - "My daughter named this puppy. If he barks, we all die."
  - "The woman who's been threatening me just saved my life. That's what scares me."
  - "Every night my daughter blinks I love you. Tonight she blinked SOS."

### Chapter 4: Across the Highway (days 11–16)

- **Cold open:** "I'm flying insulin across a mile and a half of the dead, and the battery says nine percent."
- **Story:**
  - The only way across is a drone from the electronics store. The store is on Vera's side of the mall, and only Kip knows how to set the drone up for that distance.
  - Danny has to make a deal: water and the pharmacy for the drone and Kip.
  - Vera's price is the last key. Danny gives her a key with a B3 tag on it. It's the key to a janitor's closet.
  - The two groups start living side by side, including two night cleaners Vera's crew found hiding, Ana and her son Luis.
- **Danny's choice:** he opens a door to his enemy to save his mom, and he trusts one of her crew. He's starting to lead.
- **Set piece:**
  - The flight, at nine. The buzzing pulls a river of the dead across the highway behind it.
  - Battery math, and wind.
  - The tenth-floor window opens. On the camera, Danny sees Lily for the first time in eleven days. She's holding up a new drawing: "I KNEW IT WAS YOU."
  - The insulin is delivered.
- **Cost:** the river of the dead that followed the drone is now standing around Lily's building.
- **Reveal:** Kip tells Danny everything:
  - two years of planning;
  - the cameras they tapped, which is how Vera knew his name;
  - the legend of the Maplecrest vault: a failed bank's missing millions, supposedly sealed behind B3;
  - the fourth member of the crew, whom Vera locked out to the dead on night one. Danny knows that move. He made it too.
- **Ends:** Vera on the radio: "This key opens a mop closet, Danny." A long pause. "Okay. My turn."
- **Shorts hooks:**
  - "I flew insulin across a highway full of the dead to save my mom."
  - "I hadn't seen my daughter in eleven days. Then she held up a drawing to a drone."
  - "I gave a thief a fake key. She just found out."
  - "The drone that saved my mom led a thousand dead people to her door."

### Chapter 5: The Fall of the King (days 17–25)

- **Cold open:** "Three weeks ago, I had a key to every store in this mall. Tonight I have one, and it's tied to a dog's collar."
- **Story:**
  - The groups live side by side, and Vera is good at it. She organizes, cooks and makes people laugh, and Danny starts to like her.
  - Then the broadcast changes: "Rescue teams will no longer reach all areas. Proceed to Fort Halvorsen. Children and their guardians will be evacuated first."
  - The generator runs dry and the freezers fail. The smell from the food court pulls more of the dead in from outside.
  - That night, Boone pins Danny down and Vera takes the keyring.
- **Set piece:**
  - Vera gets down to B3 and finds key 42 isn't on the ring. Danny hid it on Pancake's collar weeks ago.
  - While she's down there, the north glass gives and the dead flood the mall.
  - Kip dies getting Theo up into the ceiling.
  - Vera and Boone escape through the loading dock in a delivery truck and leave everyone else behind.
- **Danny's low point:** every door he opened got someone killed. He seals himself, Theo and the others in the security office and stops answering anyone. Lock it and walk away.
- **Ends:** at nine he climbs to the roof. No light. The next night, no light. The third night, nothing. "My daughter has never missed a night."
- **Shorts hooks:**
  - "Tonight I have one key left, and it's tied to a dog's collar."
  - "I let her in. She took every key I own."
  - "The radio just told us rescue isn't coming. Unless you have a kid."
  - "My daughter has blinked I love you every night for twenty-two days. Tonight, nothing."

### Chapter 6: Key Forty-Two (days 26–30)

- **Cold open:** "The man who gave me these keys said never to open this door. I'm about to open it."
- **Story:**
  - Theo drags Danny back: "Marcus said the king's still in his castle. Castles have doors, man."
  - In the security office, Danny finds Walt's old logbook: "B3. Civil defense shelter, 1962. Tunnel runs under Route 9 to the storm drains. Sealed after the '81 flood. Don't open it unless you have to."
  - There's no vault and no money. Vera spent two years planning to rob a hole in the ground. But it goes under the highway.
- **Set piece, part one: one last round.**
  - The dead fill the hallway to B3.
  - Danny opens the gate he wouldn't open on night one and walks out to Marcus with Marcus's radio playing. He leads Marcus, and all of them, on one last loop of the mall.
  - Then he gives his friend peace and tucks the twenty back in his pocket: "We're even."
- **Set piece, part two: the tunnel.**
  - Key 42 turns. Behind the door: black water, sixty-year-old shelves, and a tunnel a mile and a half long.
  - Danny goes first, with Theo, Pancake, Ana and Luis behind him. He leaves the key in the lock for whoever comes next.
  - Halfway through, they find a fresh can of beans and a warm camp stove. Somebody's living down here. *(That's for Book Two.)*
- **Ends:**
  - They climb out across the highway. Ten flights up, the door's open.
  - Lita is on the couch with a broken ankle, alive.
  - "Someone blinked your signal from the parking lot, mijo. Three times. Lily thought it was you. She ran before I could stop her. I fell on the stairs."
  - Vera took a seven-year-old as her ticket onto an evacuation flight, ninety miles away at Fort Halvorsen.
- **Last line:** "Thirty days ago, I had a key to every door in that mall. Now my daughter's ninety miles away with the most dangerous person I've ever met. So I'm going to go get her."
- **Shorts hooks:**
  - "My best friend's been dead for a month. Tonight I took him on one last round."
  - "I opened the door I was told never to open. It went under the highway."
  - "She spent two years planning to rob this vault. There was nothing in it."
  - "She used my daughter's own goodnight signal to take her."

## Extra chapters, if the series takes off

These slot in between Chapters 4 and 5 (days 17–22) without changing the ending. Each one is a single door:
- **Lily's Birthday.** Day twenty is Lily's eighth birthday. Danny opens the toy store and flies her a present across the highway.
- **The Knock.** A family bangs on the loading dock at 3 a.m., and one of them might be bitten. Do you open it?
- **The Cinema.** Theo's old hideout. Two hundred seats, pitch dark, and not empty.
- **The Arcade.** The loudest room in the mall becomes their best weapon, because every machine is a decoy.

## Book Two and beyond
