# The Night Guard, Chapter 1: Shorts 1–3

These are segments 1–3 of Chapter 1 in `../series-outline.md`, made as standalone vertical Shorts (1080×1920).

Each Short follows the outline's anatomy:
- a hook in the first line;
- an anchor tag on screen;
- one problem and one turn;
- a cliff that asks a question.

They use the approved voice (`../voice-recipe.md`). Lines marked **RADIO** are Marcus through the walkie-talkie. Everything else is Danny. Levels follow the recipe: 0 is the story level, −8 is the danger-close level, and so on.
