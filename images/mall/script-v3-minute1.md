# Mall pilot v3: the first minute (test)

A one-minute test of a quicker, less dramatic telling, after feedback on v2 ("a little too dramatic; engaging but quicker tempo; it has to keep people's attention"; "Marcus sounds like a girl").

- **Tempo:** about 170 words a minute, short sentences, small pauses. Danny tells it like a confident storyteller with dry humor, not like a horror trailer.
- **Hook first:** still opens on the worst moment, but stated plainly, then rewinds.
- **Voices:** Danny is Algenib (Gemini), directed brisk and conversational. Marcus is now Orus, a deeper male voice (about 114 Hz against Puck's 185–220 Hz).

Lines marked **[RADIO: Marcus]** are played through a walkie-talkie filter.

---

There's a zombie pressed against my gate. And it's wearing my best friend's uniform.

The radio on his chest is about to tell every one of them where I'm hiding.

An hour ago, I was the king of this mall. Let me back up.

I'm Danny. Night security at Maplecrest Mall. Yes, I've heard every mall cop joke.

I work nights for one reason: at three-fifteen every afternoon, I'm at the bus stop when my daughter Lily gets off the bus.

She's seven, and she thinks I'm the king of the mall. Because I carry forty-two keys. One for every store. Tonight, I'd need exactly one.

The job is simple. Walk the loop. Rattle the gates. And at one a.m., take a call from Marcus down in the parking garage.

Marcus is twenty-two, he's studying to be a nurse, and he tells the worst jokes on Earth.

Tonight he calls at one-forty. No joke. He's whispering.

**[RADIO: Marcus]** Danny. There's a guy on the ramp. He's walking... wrong.
