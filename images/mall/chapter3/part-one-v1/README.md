# Chapter 3: The Pharmacy — Part 1 artwork

Thirty selected original PNG paintings are in `public/mall/ch3/story-art/part-one-v1/`: twenty-three story scenes, six location masters, and Pancake’s character reference. All were made with the built-in image generator from approved series artwork. No KIE key, voice generation or video render is used in this stage.

Start with `preview-selected-scenes.jpg`, then the two `preview-story-*.jpg` sheets, `preview-locations.jpg` and `preview-cast.jpg`. `scene-guide.md` maps every story image to its script beat. The source manuscript, three extracted part scripts, estimated part lengths, art direction, generation/refinement prompts, file paths and SHA-256 checksums are included.

The three endings use existing script prose:

1. Approximately 11.3 minutes: “Tomorrow I’m going to use him.”
2. Approximately 11.8 minutes: “Leaving him would have saved me. I opened the cage.”
3. Approximately 10 minutes: Lily’s SOS and the new insulin a highway away.

These estimates await the established recorded voices. Narration alignment, native text/signal overlays and final scene timing belong to the subsequent video build. Use a 5–10 second visual cadence and no more than three uses of any painting.

Continuity was reviewed and corrected: Danny’s missing right boot, doubled socks only in the day-eight cold open, navy backpack, Pancake’s ear shape/collar timing, Theo’s same bandaged calf, no dead inside Summit, no premature Vera reveal, Marcus’s stickered locker and the dry fountain on the camera feed. Character and environment art share the same shaded, textured painting style.

`save-assets.py` archives selected imagegen outputs without changing their pixels. `build-review.py` validates the originals and makes contact sheets plus a portable 1920×1080 JPEG review pack under the workspace outputs folder. Native PNG masters remain in the repository for production.
