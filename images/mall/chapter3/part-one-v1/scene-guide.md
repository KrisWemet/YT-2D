## Later edit requirements

- Follow the recorded words, changing the visual every 5–10 seconds. Keep each original painting to at most three uses, including reframes. The artwork stage has no final cut timings; add additional matched inserts if the performance requires them, instead of looping a painting beyond the limit.
- Use a wide, medium or detail camera only when the necessary face, prop, foot or wound stays visible. Do not mirror shots to reframe: this reverses footwear and the bandaged calf.
- Time the Lily/Danny/department-store signal flashes to the existing spoken words; the roof painting is a static base, not a baked flash animation. Vera remains offscreen throughout Part 1.
- Keep camera labels, stopwatch digits and longer textbook margin/medical text as native readable overlays tied to the source script. The book art intentionally avoids inventing medical microtext. The sticky-note painting already reads “Told you I’d pay you back.”
- The colder Chapter 1/2 paintings can supply exact story memories (Marcus studying, back-to-back at the gate, Lily’s signal), but never use night imagery as a literal day-eight noon shot or show Pancake in Summit before the rescue.
- These assets do not include narration or a video export. Preserve the approved KIE voice profiles when production reaches audio: Danny/Algenib, Theo/Puck and Vera/Gacrux. This artwork stage used the built-in image generator and no KIE API calls.
